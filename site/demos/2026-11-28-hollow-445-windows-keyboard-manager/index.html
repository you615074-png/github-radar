How people actually use it

Three setups that show up again and again in the keyboard community — all buildable in a couple of minutes with the editor above.

🧑‍💻

Developers

Home-row mods plus a symbols layer. Hold A S D F for modifiers, hold Space for brackets, braces and operators.

Try it: click “Home-row mods”, then press A.

✍️

Writers & editors

Caps becomes Esc on tap and a navigation layer while held, so you can jump around a document without leaving the home row.

Try it: hold Caps — then press H J K L.

🎛️

Compact keyboards

Map a 60% board to your muscle memory: arrows on HJKL, page keys on the right hand, media keys on the number row.

Try it: switch to the Navigation tab.
