    Use cases
    
      
    Built for the people who ask “who is this?”
    
      
    Three teams run Tracehound every week. Same one-field workflow, three very different reports.
    
    
    

    
    
      
    
        
    🛡️
    
        
    Pre-engagement OSINT
    
        
    
          Red teams and pentesters map a target's public footprint before scoping a job — personal blogs,
          package maintainer accounts, forum handles — and attach the export to the engagement file.
        
    
        
    output: engagement-scope.pdf
    
      
    

      
    
        
    📋
    
        
    Background & due diligence
    
        
    
          Recruiters, legal teams and M&A analysts confirm which public accounts belong to a candidate or
          counterparty, and keep a timestamped record of what was public at the time.
        
    
        
    output: diligence-log.xlsx
    
      
    

      
    
        
    🕵️
    
        
    Brand & impersonation watch
    
        
    
          Add your brand handles to a watchlist and get alerted when a new account appears on a
          marketplace, forum or social platform with a near-identical name.
        
    
        
    output: takedown-queue.csv
