Use cases

Built for the people who have to ship the ad by Friday

🎯

Paid social teams

A competitor is scaling a winning ad and you have 48 hours to answer it. Get every shot classified — then know which three you can film this afternoon and which two you can generate tonight.

Typical: 15s TikTok ad → 7 shots → 2 shoot days cut to 1

✂️

Content & brand marketing

Decide whether a reference is filmable in-house, worth paying a model for, or cheaper to build in code — before the budget is locked. The remake plan splits work into film / generate / code.

Typical: 70s product film → 12 shots → 83% code-built

⚡

Creators & small agencies

Turn a viral reference into a shot list you can hand to a one-person crew: camera positions, lighting notes, caption timing and a prompt for every AI shot.

Typical: 19s mixed clip → 10 shots → 3 generations, 1 shoot
