Where it earns its keep

    
Three jobs people ask for most — each one mirrors a real prompt from the skill’s docs.

    

      

        
🎬

        
Hero entrances on landing pages

        
Paste the URL of a hero you like. It reads the stagger, easing and travel of every element, then writes it into the component you point at.

        
/reanimate the hero entrance on https://example.com, into components/Hero.tsx

      

      

        
📼

        
Clips, GIFs and Lottie files

        
When there is no DOM to inspect, it reads the frames instead. Give it a time range from a screen recording and it works the timing out from pixels.

        
/reanimate this loader, 0:03 to 0:05 of ~/Downloads/loader.mov

      

      

        
🧊

        
Canvas and WebGL

        
For shader work it traces the GPU calls on a virtual clock, so every capture is identical — then diffs yours against the original frame by frame.

        
/reanimate the cube in the resend.com hero, match it exactly
