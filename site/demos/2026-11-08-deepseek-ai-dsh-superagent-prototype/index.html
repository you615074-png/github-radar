Where it earns its keep

Small teams don't have a spare engineer for the queue of twenty-minute fixes. Relay takes that queue — and only that queue.

🐛

Bug triage on autopilot

Label an issue agent:ok. Relay reproduces it, adds a failing-then-passing test, and opens a PR with the fix explained in the description.

Median in sample runs: 6 min 40 s from label to PR.

🌙

Overnight dependency sweeps

Write a scheduled rule — "every Monday, bump patch versions and drop dead lint suppressions." Wake up to a stack of small PRs and one review queue.

Teams review batches in ~15 min instead of filing tickets.

🌱

Maintainer relief for OSS

Point it at your open-source repo. Instead of answering "PRs welcome," it turns small, well-scoped contributor requests into actual reviewable patches.

Opt-in per label, so drive-by requests never wake it up.
