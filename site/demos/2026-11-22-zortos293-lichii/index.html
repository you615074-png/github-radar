Three ways people actually use it

🖥️

Couch, but in 4K

Plug the laptop into the TV. Lichii One switches to the 4K preset and maps two Pro Controllers in the right order — no menus.

✈️

Travel light

Leave the console at home. Your own dumped library and saves live on the laptop, and the launcher rebuilds the profile on any machine.

Borrow the crowd's settings

Before you press Play you see the score and the exact preset other players used to hit a stable frame rate on similar hardware.
