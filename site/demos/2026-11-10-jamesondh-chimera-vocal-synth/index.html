Where people use it

Three workflows the browser studio was built around. All shown with sample data.

🎬

Film & game scoring

Sing a scratch vocal on the piano roll, print the consonants exactly on the beat, then export a stem. Directors hear words on day one instead of a placeholder synth pad.

WAV stems · MIDI in

🦭

Creature sound design

Morph a seal into a songbird by changing the airway instead of layering samples. Two-voice syrinx, elephant trunk, alligator — all the same engine, different anatomy.

Bestiary presets · additive anatomy

🧪

Speech & voice research

Type IPA or English text and get a controllable source with no licensing attached to it. Every gesture is a parameter you can log, sweep and compare against real speakers.

IPA input · deterministic renders
